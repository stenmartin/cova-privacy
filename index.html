Cova — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026 · Contact: oliveironcova@gmail.com

Cova ("the app", "we") is a gym-training tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. The short version: Cova is local-first — your training data lives on your device, and nothing leaves it unless you choose to.

What stays on your device

By default, everything you log stays in local storage on your phone and is never transmitted:

  • Workouts, sessions, sets (weights, reps), and rest data
  • Programs you use or build
  • Body metrics, sleep entries, and non-gym activity you choose to record
  • Progress, PRs, and insights derived from the above

Uninstalling the app removes this local data. Cova does not read contacts, photos, location, or files.

What leaves your device — only when you opt in

Cova has no account by default; being signed out is the normal state. The features below are optional and each requires an action from you.

  • Account (Sign in with Google). If you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us to authenticate you; we store them with your account. We never see or store your Google password. Required only for cloud backup and program sharing.
  • Cloud backup (opt-in). While you are signed in, a copy of your training data is uploaded to our backend under your account whenever it changes, so you can restore it on a new device. Only you can access it.
  • Program sharing by code (opt-in). If you publish a program, that program's contents and your account identifier are uploaded and given a short code. Anyone who has the code can view and copy that program. Sharing is per-program and you can unpublish a code at any time. Your training data (sessions, weights, body metrics) is never included — only the program you chose to share.

We do not sell your data or use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Cova's free tier may show ads in future (they are currently turned off; see Ads below) and sends crash reports to Sentry so crashes can be fixed (see Who processes your data).

Ads

Ads are currently turned off, and the ad SDK is not started while they are. If they are turned on, Cova's free tier will be supported by ads: a single banner on the Insights screen and one full-screen ad shown after you finish a workout. Ads never appear during a set or on a personal-best moment, and never on your History. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and the rest of this section applies only while ads are on.

  • To serve ads, AdMob accesses your device's advertising ID and may use it to show personalized ads.
  • In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, Cova shows a Google consent prompt on first launch; you choose whether ads are personalized. Where you don't consent (or outside those regions where required), ads are non-personalized.
  • Remove ads: a one-time in-app purchase removes all ads permanently on your account. Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing and managed by RevenueCat, which records only your purchase status — not your training data.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. Cova does not send marketing messages.

Who processes your data

When you use the opt-in account features above, data is stored and processed by Supabase (our backend and authentication provider) on our behalf. In addition:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) receives your advertising ID to serve ads in the free tier.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play Billing (purchase processing) handle the optional "Remove ads" purchase and record your purchase status.
  • Sentry (crash diagnostics) receives a report when the app crashes or hits an error: the error and where in the code it happened, the app version, and the device model and Android version. Reports carry no account details, training data or advertising ID, and are used only to fix bugs.

No other third parties receive your data.

Your rights and controls

  • Access / export: your data is on your device; backup is a copy you control.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time, in the app or by email. See Deleting your Cova account.
  • Withdraw sharing: unpublish any shared program to stop its code from resolving.

If you are in a jurisdiction with data-protection rights (e.g. GDPR/UK GDPR), the deletion control above satisfies your right to erasure; contact us at oliveironcova@gmail.com for any other request.

Deleting your Cova account

You can delete your Cova account and its cloud data in either of two ways:

  • In the app: open Profile → Cloud backup → Delete account and confirm. Deletion happens immediately.
  • Without the app: email oliveironcova@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Cova account". We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your account (name, email address, profile picture), your cloud backup, and any programs you published by code. Nothing is kept after deletion.

What is not affected: training data stored on your phone stays there until you uninstall the app. Crash reports never contained account details, so there is nothing in them to delete.

Children

Cova is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves; the effective date above reflects the latest version.
