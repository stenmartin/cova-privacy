Cova — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026 · Contact: st3nmartin@gmail.com

Cova ("the app", "we") is a gym-training tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. The short version: Cova is local-first — your training data lives on your device, and nothing leaves it unless you choose to.

What stays on your device

By default, everything you log stays in local storage on your phone and is never transmitted:

  • Workouts, sessions, sets (weights, reps), and rest data
  • Programs you use or build
  • Body metrics, sleep entries, and non-gym activity you choose to record
  • Progress, PRs, and insights derived from the above

Uninstalling the app removes this local data. Cova does not read contacts, photos, location, or files.

What leaves your device — only when you opt in

Cova has no account by default; being signed out is the normal state. The features below are optional and each requires an action from you.

  • Account (email + password). If you create an account, we store your email address and an encrypted password to authenticate you. Required only for cloud backup and program sharing.
  • Cloud backup (opt-in). If you turn on backup, a copy of your training data is uploaded to our backend under your account so you can restore it on a new device. Only you can access it.
  • Program sharing by code (opt-in). If you publish a program, that program's contents and your account identifier are uploaded and given a short code. Anyone who has the code can view and copy that program. Sharing is per-program and you can unpublish a code at any time. Your training data (sessions, weights, body metrics) is never included — only the program you chose to share.

We do not sell your data, show third-party ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. Cova does not send marketing messages.

Who processes your data

When you use the opt-in features above, data is stored and processed by Supabase (our backend and authentication provider) on our behalf. No other third parties receive your data.

Your rights and controls

  • Access / export: your data is on your device; backup is a copy you control.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account and all associated cloud data from within the app at any time (Profile → account). This permanently removes your account, backup, and any programs you published.
  • Withdraw sharing: unpublish any shared program to stop its code from resolving.

If you are in a jurisdiction with data-protection rights (e.g. GDPR/UK GDPR), the deletion control above satisfies your right to erasure; contact us at st3nmartin@gmail.com for any other request.

Children

Cova is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves; the effective date above reflects the latest version.
